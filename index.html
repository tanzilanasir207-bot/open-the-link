We Got This.

No matter what life throws at us,
we'll take it one step at a time.

And if either one of us
ever feels like giving up on anything —
a dream, a goal, a difficult day,
or just everything for a moment...
We remind each other why we started.
We slow down when we need to.
We pick each other up when one of us falls.
And we keep going — together.
Because it isn't you against the world.
It's us, facing it together. 🫶

Through the thick, the thin,
the chaos, the quiet — all of it.

We've got this. Always one more step.
